Let's begin with **Page 01 — Onboarding / Workspace Setup**.

The purpose of this page is not just account setup. It establishes the user's **sales intelligence profile**, which will later control prospect discovery, ICP matching, opportunity scoring, signals, and AI recommendations.

### Page 01 structure

**Step 1 — Welcome**

* Workspace/company name
* What does your business sell?
* Short business description
* Primary sales objective

**Step 2 — Ideal Customer Profile**

* Target industries
* Geographic markets
* Company size
* Revenue range
* Business type
* B2B/B2C
* Preferred client characteristics

**Step 3 — Services / Products**

* Products or services offered
* Average deal value
* Target buyer roles
* Problems you solve
* Competitive advantages

**Step 4 — Hunting Preferences**

* Hiring signals
* Funding
* Expansion
* Leadership changes
* Technology changes
* News
* Compliance/regulatory events
* Other custom signals

**Step 5 — AI Configuration**

* Aggressiveness of prospect discovery
* Opportunity scoring sensitivity
* Research depth
* Outreach personalization level

### Final screen

The user sees:

> **Your hunting system is ready.**

Then a summary:

**ICP:** Technology companies
**Location:** United States
**Company size:** 50–500
**Target buyers:** CTO, CIO, Head of Engineering
**Priority signals:** Hiring, funding, technology changes
**Average deal:** $25K

And the primary CTA:

### **Start Hunting →**

Once completed, the system takes the user directly into the **Dashboard / Sales Command Center**.
