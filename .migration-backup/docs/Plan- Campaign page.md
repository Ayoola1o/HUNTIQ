Absolutely. For **SELL → Campaigns**, the page should be the place where the user **creates, manages, monitors, and learns from outbound campaigns**.

Unlike **Pipeline**, which manages individual opportunities/deals, **Campaigns** manages a coordinated set of outreach activities directed at multiple prospects.

# HUNTIQ — SELL → Campaigns

The documentation places **Campaigns** directly under SELL, alongside Pipeline, Outreach, Tasks and Meetings. 

The intended CRM flow is essentially:

```text
HUNT
  ↓
Find Prospects
  ↓
Research
  ↓
Signals / Score
  ↓
Contacts
  ↓
SELL → Campaign
  ↓
Outreach
  ↓
Responses
  ↓
Opportunities
  ↓
Pipeline
```

So Campaigns should be the **orchestration layer between prospect intelligence and individual outreach/pipeline activity**.

# 1. Main purpose of Campaigns

The page should answer:

> **"Which prospects am I targeting, what outreach are they receiving, how is each campaign performing, and which prospects are turning into opportunities?"**

The Campaign page should let users:

* Create campaigns
* Select target prospects
* Segment prospects
* Define campaign goals
* Build outreach sequences
* Schedule outreach
* Monitor campaign activity
* Monitor responses
* Pause/resume campaigns
* Identify interested prospects
* Convert responses into opportunities
* Measure campaign performance

The documentation places **Campaigns** in Phase 2 alongside sequences, email integration, automated follow-ups and campaign analytics, so some of these should be designed now but can be implemented progressively.

# 42. Campaign execution architecture

Do **not** send all campaign messages directly from the browser.

Use a background job system:

```text
User launches campaign
        ↓
Campaign status = ACTIVE
        ↓
Scheduler
        ↓
Find prospects due for next step
        ↓
Check stop conditions
        ↓
Check permissions / sending limits
        ↓
Create outreach job
        ↓
Communication provider
        ↓
Record result
        ↓
Schedule next step
```

This will be much more reliable than client-side timers.

# 52. Most important connection to the rest of HUNTIQ

Campaigns should not operate independently.

The full architecture should be:

```text
                    HUNT
                     │
          ┌──────────┼──────────┐
          ↓          ↓          ↓
       Prospects  Companies  Contacts
          │
          ↓
       Research
          │
          ↓
       Signals
          │
          ↓
    Opportunity Score
          │
          ↓
    Decision Maker
          │
          ↓
       CAMPAIGNS
          │
          ↓
       OUTREACH
          │
       ┌──┴───┐
       ↓      ↓
    Reply   No Reply
       │
       ↓
    Meeting
       │
       ↓
   OPPORTUNITY
       │
       ↓
    PIPELINE
       │
    ┌──┴──┐
    ↓     ↓
   WON   LOST
```

This is the important distinction:

**HUNT finds the people.**
**Research understands them.**
**Signals tell you why now.**
**Campaigns coordinate the pursuit.**
**Outreach executes the communication.**
**Pipeline manages the commercial opportunity.**

That gives the **SELL → Campaigns** page a clear role in the HUNTIQ system rather than making it just another generic email-campaign screen.
