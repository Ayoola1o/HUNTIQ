Yes. I understand the direction, and I think there is a **much stronger product here than a conventional CRM**.

I checked the video you sent. The accessible transcript is centered around the entrepreneurial journey from a small starting point to building a large company, with recurring themes around **execution, customer understanding, networking, adaptability, positioning, and turning information into action**. ([Glasp][1])

What you're describing is essentially:

> **An AI-powered Client Hunting / Sales Intelligence CRM that helps a business discover potential clients, investigate them, identify opportunities, find the right decision-makers, and turn that intelligence into an outreach campaign.**

## 1. The product I would build

I wouldn't position it simply as:

**"CRM for managing clients."**

I'd position it as:

### **AI Client Intelligence & Prospecting Platform**

The workflow becomes:

**Find → Investigate → Score → Understand → Contact → Track → Convert**

Instead of the user manually researching 100 companies, your SaaS does the intelligence work.

# 20. The product's biggest challenge

The UI isn't actually the hardest part.

The hard part is **data acquisition + data quality + legal/compliant enrichment + useful signal detection**.

You don't want the application to become:

> "AI generated a nice description of a company."

You want:

> **"Here are 37 companies you can realistically sell to, here is why they need you, here is the person to approach, here is the evidence, and here is what you should say."**

That is the product.

And the philosophy from the video actually fits this well: **execution and turning information into action** are more valuable than simply having another database of companies. ([Glasp][1])
